✨ Core Features

Syllabus Progress Tracker

Track topics and subjects across 7 GATE branch presets (CS, DA, EC, EE, CE, ME, CH) with automatic completion percentages.

Deep Focus Workspace

Pomodoro, Ultradian, and Count-Up study focus timers featuring real-time clock overlays and focus history logs.

Study Analytics & Stats

Monitor your study focus hours, daily prep consistency streaks, and subject completion trends over time.

Notice Board Tasks

Organize daily study tasks with pending badge counters, smooth animations, and quick checklist management.

Live Exam Countdown

Real-time countdown timer configured for your target GATE exam date, accompanied by daily motivational quotes.

Offline SQLite Storage

100% offline-first local database storage on your device, with optional Google Authentication for cloud backup.
