📖 User Guide & Documentation

Getting Started with GATEletics

  1. Onboarding Setup: Choose your target GATE branch (CS, DA, EC, EE, CE, ME, CH), define your daily study focus goal (hours), and select your target GATE exam date.
  2. Syllabus Tracking: Expand categories to mark topics as completed. Your percentage completion rolls up automatically with atomic precision.
  3. Focus Mode: Select Pomodoro (25m/5m), Ultradian (90m/20m), or Count-Up mode. Review real-time clock overlays (`07:44 PM`) and save completed sessions to your stats history.
  4. Cloud Sync & Backups: Sign in with Google to enable automatic offline-first cloud backups. Data is saved locally first and backed up silently in the background.
